# Online Store Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Project Preview

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard-preview.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Online Store Sales & Profit Analysis dashboard** developed using **Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer trends, and regional business performance.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

- 📈 Sales and revenue performance
- 💰 Profit and profitability analysis
- 📅 Monthly and yearly sales trends
- 📦 Product and category performance
- 👥 Customer analysis
- 🌍 Regional sales performance
- 🎯 KPI monitoring
- 📊 Business performance insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

`Power BI` `Power Query` `DAX` `Excel` `SQL`

## 🎓 Skills Demonstrated

- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Modeling
- DAX Measures & Calculated Columns
- Sales & Profit Analysis
- KPI Development
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence
- Business Analysis
- Trend Analysis
- Insight Generation

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify sales trends, profitable products and categories, regional performance, customer patterns, and opportunities for improving overall business profitability.

## 🚀 Project Outcome

Developed an interactive dashboard that transforms raw sales data into meaningful **business insights and KPI-driven visualizations** to support data-driven decision-making.
